Our Journey & Mission

Headquartered at Loknath Market, Jagatpur, Baguiati (Kolkata – 700159), YouS2 was established with a singular focus: to close the gap between creative visual media, premium customized merchandise, banking & loan support, and daily statutory business utilities under one seamless roof.

In an era of mass-produced, impersonal goods, we believe every tangible print should carry character. Whether capturing a couple's cinematic wedding story, printing high-durability DTF apparel, engineering UV-printed acrylic displays, or assisting local entrepreneurs with Trade Licenses, MSME registrations, and Business Loans, our team brings meticulous craftsmanship to every project.

Visual Storytelling

From 4K cinematic event videography to luxury lay-flat photo albums and instant studio portraits, we document memories with modern artistry.

Cutting-Edge Customization

Equipped with Direct-to-Film (DTF), UV curing, and computerized thread embroidery, we transform everyday apparel and drinkware into bespoke keepsakes.

Commercial Branding

We empower growing businesses with glow sign boards, roll-up standees, luxury visiting cards, and self-inking stamps engineered to attract attention.

Citizen, Banking & E-Gov Services

Reliable administrative assistance covering PAN card updates, Trade License processing, Business Current Accounts, and Loan assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about placing orders, studio bookings, and turnaround times.

Where is YouS2 studio located, and do you accept walk-ins?

We are located at YouS2, Loknath Market, Jagatpur, Aswini Nagar, Baguiati, Kolkata – 700159. Walk-ins are always welcome during business hours!

What is the average turnaround time for personalized gifts & apparel?

Most standard custom apparel and DTF prints are ready within 24 to 48 hours. Express same-day options are available for urgent requests.

Can I review digital mockups before printing begins?

Yes! We share digital proofs/mockups over WhatsApp for your approval before final production starts.

Do you provide official GST invoices for corporate orders?

Yes, 100% compliant B2B tax invoices with valid GSTIN are provided for all corporate and business print orders.

What government documentation and banking services do you assist with?

Through sortnfix and our partner network, we assist with MSME registration, Trade Licenses, GST filings, PAN cards, ITR filing, Business Current Accounts, and Business/Personal/Mortgage Loans.
